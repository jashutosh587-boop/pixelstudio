Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In plain language: PixelCraft Studio is a browser-based image editor. The image files you select for editing are processed locally in your browser by the tools in this version; they are not uploaded to a PixelCraft image-processing server. The site does load third-party resources, including the Google AdSense library, and those providers may receive standard connection information when your browser requests them.

1. Image files and local processing

PixelCraft Studio uses browser image features, including Canvas and image decoding APIs, to crop, resize, adjust, convert and export images on your device. The image tools in this version do not send the selected image files to a PixelCraft image-processing server. Images are held in browser memory while you edit them. Downloads and project files are created by your browser when you choose to save them.

Files you download or save remain wherever your browser or device stores them. Use care when editing sensitive images on a shared or public device.

2. Information handled to operate the website

PixelCraft Studio is hosted as a static website. The hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, request time, browser and device information, and the page requested in server logs for delivery, security and reliability. The hosting provider's own privacy terms govern its processing.

The site loads interface resources from third-party content delivery networks, including Tailwind CSS and Lucide icons. When your browser requests these resources, the providers may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, browser details and the requested resource. These requests do not send the image files you edit through PixelCraft's local tools.

3. Local preferences and consent controls

The site includes a preference panel for essential, analytics and advertising categories. The current version stores the selected preference in your browser's local storage under a PixelCraft preference key. Essential storage is used to remember the preference. You can reopen the panel using Cookie settings in the site footer, change your selection, or clear the site's stored data through your browser settings.

The preference panel records your selection and exposes it to the site's scripts. It is not, by itself, a certified consent-management platform (CMP). In the current code, the Google AdSense library is included in the homepage regardless of the advertising toggle; the toggle does not currently prevent that library from being requested. Do not treat the advertising toggle as a guarantee that all third-party advertising requests are blocked. The site operator should implement and verify an appropriate consent solution and ensure optional tags respect applicable consent requirements before enabling ad delivery.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

The homepage currently includes the Google AdSense JavaScript library associated with publisher account pub-2287896200203519. The visible advertising areas in this version are placeholders; no live ad unit is currently configured in those spaces. Loading the AdSense library can nevertheless cause your browser to request resources from Google. Google may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser or device information, and page/request information as part of providing and operating its services.

If advertising is enabled in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to store or access information on your browser, deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted and based on your choices and applicable settings, personalize advertising. Google's use of information is subject to Google's own policies and settings. Learn more in Google's advertising technologies information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Visitors can review Google's My Ad Center and browser controls for available advertising and cookie choices. Those controls do not replace any consent mechanism that the site operator is legally required to provide. The site operator must keep this policy and the live consent configuration accurate as advertising features change.

5. Analytics

This version does not include a Google Analytics tag or a separate analytics SDK in the website source reviewed for this policy. The preference panel includes an analytics option, but selecting it does not itself activate an analytics service. This section will be updated if an analytics provider is added.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may use the editor without creating a PixelCraft account. You can change the preference recorded by the site's settings panel or clear local site data through your browser. Because the AdSense library is currently loaded independently of the advertising toggle, that toggle alone does not guarantee that the library request is prevented.
