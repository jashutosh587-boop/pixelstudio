Image output and quality

Results depend on the source image, browser, device, selected dimensions, output format and quality settings. Resizing or compression may reduce detail, and conversion may change file size or appearance. Review each output before publishing, printing, submitting or relying on it. Keep a copy of important originals.

Passport and ID photographs

Passport and ID photo presets create an image using the selected physical dimensions and DPI. They do not detect or validate face position, head size, expression, lighting, background, image age or any authority's current requirements. Photo rules vary by country, document and application. Check the official instructions for the relevant authority before using an output.

Background removal

The included background remover uses color sampling and chroma-key processing. It is not AI subject segmentation and may leave artifacts or remove parts of a subject that are similar in color to the sampled background.
